What does AI do well?

In this activity you will sort a set of everyday tasks according to whether artificial intelligence systems are able to do them well, or whether they tend to struggle with them. Read each task carefully, decide which category it belongs to, and drag it into the correct column. When you have sorted all of the tasks, click the Check answers button to see how you did.

Drag the cards from the Task tray into one of the two trays: “AI does this well” or “AI struggles here.”

Key takeaways
  • ✓AI is strong at tasks with lots of training examples and clear patterns.
  • ✓AI is weak at tasks needing true understanding, reasoning, or current information.
  • ✓"Feels smart" and "is reliable" are not the same thing.
  • ✓Always check AI output for the kinds of tasks in the "struggles" column.
